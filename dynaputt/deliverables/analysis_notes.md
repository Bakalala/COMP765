# Experiment analysis notes

Everything here is regenerated by `run_experiments.py`, `summarize_results.py`, and
`run_extended.py`. Three datasets exist and are never pooled:

| Dataset | Location | Seeds | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | `results/` | 11, 23, 37, 41, 59 | Pre-planned comparison, separate streams |
| Archive | `results/rng_coupled_baseline/` | same | Earlier exploratory run, shared stream |
| Extended | `results/extended/` | 1000-1099 | Robustness and exploratory studies |

## Metric definitions

- **Threshold episode:** first episode ending a 20-episode window with at least 16
  training sinks. Training includes exploration.
- **Real shots to threshold:** training shots up to that episode. This is the metric
  closest to the research question.
- **Greedy success, episodes 301-350:** mean over the 50 checkpoints and five seeds,
  so 250 deterministic rollouts from only five training histories. The seed is the
  replication unit.
- **Greedy-stable episode:** first episode after which all checkpoints sink, counted
  only if at least 50 checkpoints remain.

## Extended results

**Budget.** Threshold on Easy for k = 0, 1, 2, 5, 10, 20, 50 is 80.3, 64.3, 60.4,
56.9, 54.3, 53.5, 52.7; on Wall-gate 281.3, 178.1, 141.3, 101.9, 91.8, 87.3, 85.0.
k=1 gives more than half of the total gain.

**5 versus 20.** Easy gap 3.4 episodes (95% CI 2.1 to 4.7), k=20 faster in 64 of 100
seeds, 7 ties. Wall-gate gap 14.6 (13.0 to 16.3), faster in 99 of 100. The primary
Easy gap of 11.8 and its five-of-five result were favorable draws.

**Stream.** Easy thresholds at k=5 and k=20 are 56.9 and 53.5 with separate streams,
56.4 and 52.7 with a shared stream. Five-seed resamples put k=5 ahead on Easy 12.2%
and 21.1% of the time. So the archive's reversal is best explained by sampling noise.
Do not call it protocol sensitivity.

**Initial Q.** k=0 minus k=20 threshold difference, with 95% CI:

| Course | Q=0 | Q=-5 | Q=-20 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Easy | 26.7 (24.7, 28.6) | -0.4 (-1.7, 0.9) | -0.1 (-1.2, 0.9) |
| Wall-gate | 194.0 (190.4, 197.6) | 37.6 (35.3, 39.8) | 30.2 (25.7, 35.0) |

On Easy at Q=-5 and -20, threshold is floored by exploration (epsilon is near 0.8 at
episode 30) and the greedy policy is stable by about episode 4 with or without
planning. On Wall-gate greedy stability still improves, from 93.2 to 31.8 at Q=-5 and
94.6 to 53.0 at Q=-20.

**Route length (800 episodes).** Best routes of 3, 4, 7, and 8 shots.

| Initial Q | Threshold speedup k=0 to k=20 | Greedy-stable speedup | Seeds at threshold, k=0 / k=20 |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 0 | 2.3x, 2.0x, 3.1x, 3.7x | 3.1x, 5.0x, 5.7x, 5.5x | all 100, all routes |
| -5 | 1.3x, 1.2x, 1.7x, 2.1x | 3.1x, 3.4x, 3.0x, 3.1x | all 100, all routes |
| -20 | 1.1x, 1.1x, n/a, n/a | 2.1x, 2.4x, n/a, n/a | 100/100, 100/100, 90/98, 6/32 |

The Q=0 greedy-stable values use the seeds that qualify: 87 of 100 at k=0 for the
7-shot route and 98 of 100 for the 8-shot route.

## Interpretation

- H1 (fewer shots) and H2 (20 beats 5) are supported in the primary run and on average
  at 100 seeds, under the zero initialization.
- H3 (exploratory) is supported: most of the gain on short routes depends on the
  optimistic table.
- H4 (exploratory) is partly supported: absolute savings and threshold speedup grow
  with route length at Q=-5, but the greedy-stable speedup stays near 3x.
- Optimism has a benefit too: Q=-20 mostly fails on the 8-shot course. The mechanisms
  (optimism draining, value propagation) are interpretations and were not measured.

## Claims to avoid

- That more planning always helps by a similar amount, or that the effect is general.
- That Dyna-Q beats Q-learning in general: well-initialized Q-learning is competitive.
- That non-optimistic initialization is better in general.
- That the shared stream caused the archived reversal.
- Statistical claims beyond these seeds and courses.
- Any claim about real-world control or neural world models.
